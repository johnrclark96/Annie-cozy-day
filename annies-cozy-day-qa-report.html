UX Concerns (5)
UX-1: First-visit overlay stacking is confusing — Dedication and daily gift overlays should be sequential, not simultaneous. Player's first interaction feels broken. (Related to BUG-027)
UX-2: Diminishing returns on rapid petting not communicated — No visual feedback (cooldown bar, reduced particles) when petting effectiveness decreases.
UX-3: Game descriptions in minigames menu use small text — Could be difficult to read for younger users or smaller displays.
UX-4: Minigame instruction dismiss text is small — "Click to begin early · Esc to go back" easily missed by players.
UX-5: No escape route from results screen if Back button fails — No Escape key support. Player is trapped. (Related to BUG-045)
